SkyMissions

Review your active character, manage owned homes and name cards, and earn SkyPoints through randomized missions.

Character record

SkyMissions includes a dashboard with faction and property management, a twelve-offer randomized mission board arranged in four desktop columns, and a SkyStore for point purchases. Mission offers can target Afflicted, bandits, Forsworn, ghosts, Reavers, warlock mage types, Penitus Oculatus, vampires, witches, hostile creatures, named ingredients, potion crafting, specific potions, and general or named smithing tasks. SkyRewrite and SkyCollection provide optional history and discovery data.
